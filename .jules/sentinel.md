## 2026-10-11 - SSRF via Open Redirects in Image Downloads and OAuth Token fetching
**Vulnerability:** In `apps.products.admin_serializers` and `apps.accounts.views`, when fetching remote images for products/categories and fetching Google OAuth tokeninfo, the `requests.get` call lacked domain validation, scheme validation, and allowed redirects by default. This allowed an attacker to bypass intended host restrictions and scan internal services via SSRF.
**Learning:** You must restrict the protocol to HTTPS, strictly validate the `hostname` against an allowlist, AND disable redirects (`allow_redirects=False`) to prevent open redirect chaining attacks.
**Prevention:** Always use a 3-part check: `parsed.scheme == "https"`, `parsed.hostname in allowed_hosts`, and `allow_redirects=False` in `requests.get()`.
