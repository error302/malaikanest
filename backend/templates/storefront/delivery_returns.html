Returns

  • Unused items in their original condition and packaging can be returned within 7 days of delivery.
  • Message us on WhatsApp at +254 726 771 321 with your order number to arrange a return.
  • For hygiene reasons, items worn, washed, or marked cannot be accepted.
  • Refunds are processed to your M-Pesa or PayPal account within 1–3 business days after we receive and check the item.
  • If an item arrives damaged or is not what you ordered, we will replace it or refund it in full — no questions asked.
